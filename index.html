Can Math

The 468 cans at 24 cans a carton take 20 cartons, the last carton holding 12 cans.

Boundary: arithmetic only Can Math counts an event's soda cans with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the cartons the cans take, the weeks the stock lasts, the cost a can, and the minutes the stacking takes. It uses no named constants. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your cans, your cartons or your cooler and does not know whether the cans will really arrive cold, the supplier will run out or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a carton is worth its price or a can is right for your event. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: 468 cans, 24 cans a carton, 65 cans a week, $9.60 a carton and 15 cans a minute.

The weeks truth

The 468 cans at 65 cans a week last 7 full weeks with 13 cans left for the next week.

The cost truth

The 20 cartons at $9.60 a carton come to $192.00, $0.41 a can.

The time truth

The 468 cans at 15 cans a minute take 32 minutes, 0 hours 32 minutes.
